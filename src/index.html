Who I am

About me

I’m pursuing a BS in Computer Applications, majoring in Embedded Systems, at MSU-IIT in Iligan City. I develop embedded prototypes for sensor monitoring and activity recognition.

My current research includes an ongoing, university-funded study on detecting aphid damage using edge AI and a systematic literature review on bell-pepper disease and pest monitoring.

My experience spans real-time embedded programming, digital fabrication, online English teaching, student government, and technology events. I hope to build on this foundation through research placements abroad and collaborative projects with practical applications.
